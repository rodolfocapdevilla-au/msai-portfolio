## Project Cards

1. **Monday Supplier Dashboard (Claude Skill)** — A reusable Claude skill that takes a week of supplier meeting notes, a delivery export and a quality-incident log and turns them into one decision dashboard for a procurement committee. I wrote the procedure once and then ran it unchanged on a week of data it had never seen. It kept the same traffic-light rules and the same output shape, and it was correct both times.

2. **RAG Client-Intake Assistant for Small Law Firms** — A retrieval-augmented assistant built for Intelvora that answers routine intake questions for small Brazilian law firms using their own policy documents. I fixed a retrieval bug where it mixed up two similar policies, added an out-of-scope refusal so it says "I don't know" instead of guessing, and tested it against 11 questions with measured accuracy.

3. **Axelis AI** — My company, which builds AI-driven CRM and automation for service businesses: voice agents, client intake and follow-up, so a small business can answer every client without a bigger front desk.

4. **The AI Revenue Engine** — A business book I wrote about applying AI to revenue operations, the same mix of B2B sales, operations and practical automation that shows up in Axelis AI and in my coursework.
