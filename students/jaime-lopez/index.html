Current Projects

A web page for Café del Istmo

A one-page website in Spanish for a family coffee farm in Boquete, Panama, that sells to roasters and cafés in Miami and had no site. I built it with three Claude skills, changed the design one of them proposed so it matched the colors in the client's brief, and left out three of the client's claims because the delivery records did not support them. The result was a page with one goal, asking for a free sample box, plus a logo, a browser icon, a sharing image and a written review of what each skill does and which one I trust least.

Transcribing a class talk with Whisper

An exercise in running a published AI model, OpenAI's Whisper, to turn the audio of Tahl Milburn's class on AI ethics into text. I ran Whisper in Google Colab on the 20-minute talk and the 7-minute question session, then used Claude to summarize the transcripts and to think through my own question about what to do when ethical frameworks conflict. The result was two transcripts, a summary that marks where the speech recognition failed, and a question for Tahl that the recording does not answer.
