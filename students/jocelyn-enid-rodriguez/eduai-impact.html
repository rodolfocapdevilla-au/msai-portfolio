EduAI Impact

Empowering educators through leadership, innovation, and responsible AI.

What is EduAI Impact?

EduAI Impact is an educational consulting initiative designed to support early childhood educators and educational leaders through instructional coaching, professional development, data-informed decision-making, and the responsible integration of artificial intelligence.

My Contribution

I developed the initiative's service framework, brand identity, bilingual educational resources, and the IMPACT Method, a six-step framework designed to help educators create effective and responsible AI prompts while maintaining professional judgment and protecting privacy.

Project Outcomes

The initiative has produced bilingual visual guides, practical AI resources, professional development materials, and instructional coaching tools. EduAI Impact continues to evolve as a professional initiative connecting educational leadership, technology, and continuous improvement.
