Current Projects

EduAI Impact

EduAI Impact is an educational consulting initiative focused on early childhood leadership and the responsible use of AI. I developed its service framework, its brand identity, and bilingual resources for educators. It has produced professional development materials and practical resources for educators, and work on it continues.

The IMPACT Method

The IMPACT Method is a six-step framework for writing clear and responsible AI prompts in early childhood education. I created the framework and its bilingual teaching materials. It resulted in visual guides that remind educators to protect privacy, use their professional judgment, and check AI-generated content.

AHAVORA Records

AHAVORA Records is an independent digital music project that explores AI-assisted music production and creative entrepreneurship. I developed its brand identity, its music collections, and a production workflow that uses generative AI tools. It has produced music recordings and visual branding, and a digital catalog is in development.

Data-Driven Instructional Coaching

This professional initiative uses educational assessment data to guide instructional coaching and continuous improvement. I analyze CLASS observation results, identify instructional priorities, and design coaching strategies for specific needs. This work has produced data summaries, professional development resources, and individualized coaching plans.
