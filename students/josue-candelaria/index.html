Classroom Rewards

A reward store for the classroom that runs in a single web page. Students earn points, spend them on prizes the teacher chooses, and try their luck on prize wheels. I built it as one file that needs no installation, accounts, or internet.

Typing Quest

A typing practice battle game for 5th and 7th graders. Students fight enemies and bosses by typing words and sentences, and matching every capital letter exactly lands a critical hit. It runs as one file with no installation, accounts, or server. Play it online.

Math Steps

An interactive math app for elementary school students, in English and Spanish. It teaches place value, adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing one step at a time, with animations that show what happens when you carry, borrow and bring down. It needs no installation or account. Try it online.
