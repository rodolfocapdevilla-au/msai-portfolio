## The loop
Read → judge → choose one thing → change → look again → decide.

## Stopping rule

The rule "Stop when the page is good." has no test, so it was replaced with this one:

- **Bar:** all 5 checks in `RUBRIC.md` pass, each with one sentence of quoted evidence. Check the page as a phone reader sees it, because that is the reader in `GOAL.md`.
- **Budget:** at most 3 passes of the loop.
- **Plateau:** stop early if one pass does not flip any failing check from fail to pass.
- If the budget or the plateau is reached before the bar is met, stop anyway and say, in the pull request, exactly which checks still fail. Do not hide a failure to look finished.
