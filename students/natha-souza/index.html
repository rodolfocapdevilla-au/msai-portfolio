Current Projects

ChemSafe RAG — a local AI assistant for chemical process safety

A small language model that answers safety questions from SDS sheets and OSHA/NIOSH excerpts, cites the section it used, and says so when the documents do not cover the question. My MAI 600 capstone: citation accuracy rose from 82% to 94%.

Bearing Fault Classification — vibration signals from the CWRU dataset

I classified four bearing conditions from raw vibration data, training at three motor loads and testing on a fourth load with added noise, so the score reflects conditions the models never saw. A Random Forest and a ResNet18 transfer-learning model both reached about 85%.

Local Model Bake-off — three open-weight LLMs on a laptop

I ran Llama 3.2 3B, Qwen 2.5 3B and Gemma 2 2B through Ollama on a CPU-only laptop, timed them and scored their summaries of lab incident reports. Llama 3.2 3B was the most consistent and the fastest.

ML Trading Signal System — calibrated XGBoost for BTC/USDT

A walk-forward XGBoost pipeline with isotonic calibration that turns Binance market data into 15-minute trading signals, delivered by a Telegram bot. It replaced a rule-based system that had stopped working.
