AI Impact on the Spanish Labor Market

This project examined how exposure to artificial intelligence relates to employment trends in Spain. I combined Spanish labor market data with AI exposure indicators and tested eight machine learning models. Random Forest was the best-performing individual model, and I built an ensemble of three models to estimate future employment trends.
