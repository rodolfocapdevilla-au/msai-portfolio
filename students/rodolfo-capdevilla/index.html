Rodolfo Capdevilla

I am a scientist with ten years of experience doing research in high energy physics. Studying particle collider data brought me to the worlds of Machine Learning and AI. I now teach AI at Atlantis University; from simple workflows and agents to deep learning.

Current Projects

Volgo: live translation for events

A speaker talks into a phone and the audience follows translated captions and voice in their own languages. I founded it and built the system with AI coding agents. It is live, with 27 languages.

TinkerBee: a voice assistant that sees

An assistant for security and inspection work that guides a person through a task step by step. I built it on a Raspberry Pi 5 with speech recognition, camera vision and Claude. It is a working prototype in daily use.

Sharky: AI judges for startup pitches

A panel of three AI investors that listens to a pitch and answers out loud against a scoring rubric. I built it in two days. It judged five student startup pitches live at Atlantis University.

WeatherAE: rain prediction for Miami

A short term rain forecaster that learns without labelled examples. I trained 24 autoencoders, one for each hour of the day, on ten years of weather data. It runs as a Streamlit app.
