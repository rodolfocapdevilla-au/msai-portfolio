Rodolfo Roldan

I am a scientist with ten years of experience doing research in high energy physics. Studying particle collider data brought me to the worlds of Machine Learning and AI. I now teach AI at Atlantis University; from simple workflows and agents to deep learning.

Current Projects

Volgo

Volgo is a live translation tool for in-person events, where people follow a speaker on their phones in their own language. I founded the company and built the system with the help of AI coding agents. It has been online with open signup since August 2026 and works with 27 languages.

WeatherAE

WeatherAE is a project that makes short-term rain forecasts for Miami. I trained 24 autoencoders, one for each hour of the day, on ten years of Open-Meteo weather data. The result is a working app built with Streamlit.
