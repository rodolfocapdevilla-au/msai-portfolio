Current Projects

Evaluación técnico-financiera del potencial del pozo exploratorio vino 1 del campo Cupiraco mediante pruebas de presión DST

My undergraduate thesis in Petroleum Engineering (Universidad de América, 2019). I evaluated deeper formations of the Cupiraco field that had never been tested, using DST pressure tests from the exploratory well Vino 1 and the Horner method, plus PVT lab data. I then estimated reserves and potential and judged the project's financial viability with Net Present Value.
